## Incident reporting (Art. 23)

| Phase | Deadline | Trigger / content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| early_warning | 24 hours | An early warning to CERT.at / the competent authority indicating whether the incident is suspected to be caused by unlawful or malicious acts or could have a cross-border impact. |
| notification | 72 hours | An incident notification providing an initial assessment of the incident, its severity and impact and any indicators of compromise, updating and elaborating on the early warning. |
| progress_update | on CSIRT request | Status updates and any intermediate findings provided upon request of the CSIRT or the competent authority while the incident is being handled. |
| final_report | 1 month | A final report with a detailed description of the incident, its root cause, its severity and impact, the mitigation measures applied, and any cross-border impact. |
